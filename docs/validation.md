# Discovery feedback and validation

## Early problem discovery

Project correspondence dated 30 September 2026 records a colleague's account of exploratory meetings with Bihar electricity-company leadership. The reported guidance was to:

- Select one or two specific ground-level problems and establish the need before defining an AI solution.
- Explore consumer-grievance follow-up and transformer-related complaints as possible starting points.
- Begin with a small workflow and a manageable body of paperwork, given the continued use of paper records.

These are paraphrased, relayed discovery observations from before the current prototype. They informed the focus on a single grievance workflow, case history, pending actions and follow-up drafts. They are not a task-based evaluation of this version.

The source is project correspondence dated 30 September 2026. Private messages, personal contact details and direct quotations are not reproduced here.
